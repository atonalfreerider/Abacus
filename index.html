Area under the curve

Sweep the point from a to b. Each step stacks a column of card strips Δx wide up to the curve; red strips below the axis cancel blue ones. Then the columns gather into one stack of whole cards.

Equation

Exact fractions, decimals, parentheses, and linear algebra in x, for example x/2 + 1/3 = 5/6. Use × to multiply and ÷ (or :) to divide, for example 23 × 4 or 156 ÷ 12; tap the product to work it out. A slash between numbers is a fraction.

View

Tens have 10 cards; hundreds have 100. Stacks extend down and right. k, M, and B are inscribed directly on each face. In base 2 a place holds one card stack of 1, 2, 4 or 8 cards (then 2⁴, 2⁸ marks); in balanced ternary a red card is the digit −1 (written T); in base e a stack is e^k cards, its last card sliced, and the digits run on (…). Equations can also be typed with 0b1011 or 0t1T0 literals.
