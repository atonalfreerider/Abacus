Privacy

Abacus, by Meta Virtuoso · effective 12 October 2026

In short

Abacus does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It is suitable for children.

The apps (Android and iOS)

  • Everything the app shows is built into it. It makes no network requests and asks for no permissions beyond vibration, which it uses for haptic feedback.
  • The equations you type, the cards you move and your lesson progress stay on the device, in memory, and are gone when the app closes. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The app contains no third-party software development kits for advertising, analytics or crash reporting, and it does not read device identifiers.

The website

The web version is the same program, served as static files. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The company that hosts the files may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and the file requested) for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

Children

Because Abacus collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Google Play's Families policy.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does anything differently. Questions: write to the developer email shown on the Abacus page in Google Play or the App Store.
