## Full description (4000 max)
Abacus turns every number into cards you can hold. Each place has its own 3×3 tray: ones are single cards, tens are stacks of ten, hundreds are stacks of a hundred. Instead of memorising rules, you watch them happen.

ADD AND SUBTRACT WITH REAL CARDS
• When a tray fills, ten cards stack into one and slide to the next place: that is carrying.
• A stack unstacks into ten to make room: that is borrowing.
• A blue (positive) card and a red (negative) card cancel each other in a zero pair.

MULTIPLY AND DIVIDE
• Multiplication copies a number onto every card of the other factor, then adds the copies.
• Long division deals the cards into equal groups, largest place first. Leftovers unstack into the next place, or are sliced into fractions.

ORDER OF OPERATIONS (PEMDAS)
• Parentheses and exponents stay on screen until they are worked out: (5 − 3)² becomes 2², then 2 × 2 = 4.
• Then × and ÷ from the left, then + and − from the left, every step narrated.
• Six lessons, from "multiply before adding" to all of PEMDAS together.

SOLVE FOR X
• Drag a card across the equals mirror and its sign flips. Get the green x alone.
• Hints point at the card to move; "Show me" does the step; feedback tells you whether a move helped.

GRAPHING
• Drag a point along a line or curve: its x and y appear as square cards laid side by side.
• Where a line crosses an axis, one coordinate is zero. Abacus puts in the zero and solves the equation for you to see: 2(0) + 3y = 12, so y = 4.
• Find where two lines cross to solve a system, and watch card strips add up the area under a curve.

A TUTOR THAT NEVER RUNS OUT OF PROBLEMS
Every lesson plays a worked example step by step, then deals you a new problem of the same kind. The examples change every time you open the app.

MADE FOR TOUCH
• Phone and tablet layouts, portrait or landscape.
• Haptic feedback as cards snap, cancel and cross the mirror.
• Press and hold (or press firmly) on any card to peek inside it: its places as stacks of cards, or every step of a calculation still to be worked out.
• Long-press the app icon to jump straight to Lessons, PEMDAS, Graph or a new equation.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no data collection. Abacus works completely offline.

Always ask why. Understand math.
